# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pet owners, merchants, keepers, customer service staff, and administrators use this system to manage pet boarding and related service workflows. Users are usually trying to finish operational tasks quickly: browse services, place and track orders, manage pets, review applications, handle support, and monitor platform activity.

## Product Purpose

This product is a pet service and boarding management platform. It connects pet owners with merchants and keepers, supports order fulfillment and payments, and gives operators/admins the tools to review content, manage users, handle disputes, and keep the platform running.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, warm. The interface should feel reliable enough for operational work while still being friendly for pet owners.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, overly saturated orange surfaces, marketing-style hero layouts inside task screens, low-contrast tinted text, and unsafe HTML rendering for status labels or table data.

## Design Principles

1. Task clarity first: controls, tables, and forms should prioritize scanning and action.
2. Trust through consistency: shared status, button, table, and layout patterns should behave the same across roles.
3. Warm but restrained: pet-service friendliness should come from wording and small details, not heavy color washes.
4. Safe by default: user-facing or API-provided content should render as text unless explicitly structured.
5. Accessible operations: important states and actions should be readable, keyboard-visible, and usable in light and dark themes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve visible focus states, avoid motion-only feedback, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep color-coded statuses backed by readable labels.
